page: Workshop

## Purpose
Find the root cause of a slowing workshop and fix it before availability and cost follow.

## Steps
- [ ] Open the site in **Workshop → drill-down** and confirm the flag is not a low-volume week.
- [ ] Split turnaround into queue, labour and **parts wait**. The parts-wait share shows which one moved.
- [ ] Check technician coverage against last month (attrition, leave, training).
- [ ] Check first-time fix. A drop means rework is consuming capacity.
- [ ] Agree corrective actions: parts expedite, overflow to a sister site, overtime, or a vendor.
- [ ] Set a recovery target (median turnaround back under the network median) and a date.
- [ ] Re-check weekly until the site has posted **4 clean weeks**; then close the intervention.

## Exit criteria
4 consecutive weeks without INTERVENE; the clean streak shows on the Leaderboard.

## Escalation
No improvement after 3 weeks escalates to the regional director, with a capacity or vendor decision.
