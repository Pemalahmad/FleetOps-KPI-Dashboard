## Steps
- [ ] Approve the scope, nominate role holders and review risks and applicable obligations.
- [ ] Validate vehicle identity, registration class, permits and source evidence at onboarding.
- [ ] Confirm driver entitlement, contractor eligibility and journey controls before assignment.
- [ ] Apply release checks, record holds, and retain the signed release record.
- [ ] Verify maintenance, repair and recall work against the required evidence.
- [ ] Assign registration, inspection and renewal cases and verify replacements.
- [ ] Record incidents, emergency actions, required notifications and independent closure.
- [ ] Verify environmental controls, waste records and monitoring evidence.
- [ ] Assign and close complaints, supplier findings and service recovery actions.
- [ ] Record shift, weekly, monthly and management review decisions.
- [ ] Run the internal audit and independently verify corrective actions.
- [ ] Verify certificate scope and maintain surveillance and renewal dates.

## 1. Standards and certification scope

An independent certification body certifies the organization's management system for a defined scope. Record the legal entity, sites and activities exactly as issued on the certificate. Vehicle release continues to depend on the applicable GCC authority requirements, vehicle evidence and operating safety checks. [ISO certification overview](https://www.iso.org/certification.html)

| Reference | Fleet controls |
|---|---|
| ISO 39001:2012 | Road traffic risks, driver and journey controls, safe deployment, incident investigation and exposure-based safety measures. [ISO 39001](https://www.iso.org/standard/44958.html) |
| ISO 45001:2018 | Worker consultation and competence, workshop and depot hazards, contractor safety, emergency preparedness and EHS incident investigation. [ISO 45001](https://www.iso.org/standard/45001) |
| ISO 14001:2026 | Environmental impacts, spill readiness, waste and fluids, fuel and energy management and applicable environmental obligations. [ISO 14001](https://www.iso.org/standard/14001) |
| ISO 9001:2026 | Controlled procedures, supplier oversight, service quality, complaint resolution and corrective action. [ISO 9001](https://www.iso.org/standard/9001) |
| ISO 19011:2026 | Guidance for planning, running and evaluating management-system audits. [ISO 19011](https://www.iso.org/standard/19011) |

For a GCC rental or transport operator with significant driving exposure, prioritize ISO 39001 and ISO 45001. Confirm the need for ISO 14001 and ISO 9001 based on operational impacts, customer requirements, contracts and tenders. Check applicable amendments and edition transitions with the certification body.

| Role | Responsibility |

| Site or Duty Manager | Runs the shift review, applies operating holds, coordinates emergency response and approves local escalation. |
| Compliance Lead | Maintains the country obligations register, checks statutory vehicle evidence and manages the certification register. |
| EHS Lead | Maintains risk assessments, emergency plans and contractor controls. Coordinates incident reporting decisions. |
| Workshop or Maintenance Lead | Owns defects, repairs, preventive maintenance, recall execution and quality evidence. |
| Dispatch and Driver Leads | Verify driver entitlement, vehicle readiness and journey rules before authorizing dispatch. |
| Quality Lead | Controls SOP versions, supplier performance, the audit programme and corrective actions. |
| Independent Reviewer | Verifies closure evidence and effectiveness independently of the case owner. |

Maintain named contacts, deputies and decision authorities for each site and shift.

## 3. Fleet operating procedure

| Control | Trigger and owner | Steps and exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Scope and risk** | At launch and after material changes · Director, EHS and Compliance | List in-scope entities, sites, activities and contractors. Assess road, workplace and environmental risks with staff and drivers. Assign controls, objectives and review dates. Release new activities after approved risk controls are in place. Retain the scope and risk register. |
| **2. Vehicle and data onboarding** | At acquisition, transfer or import · Fleet Data Analyst and Compliance | Match fleet ID, plate and VIN where relevant. Confirm the official registration class, jurisdiction, site and intended use. Reconcile duplicates, missing records, registration, insurance, inspections, permits and recalls. Store the evidence references and route gaps to review or hold. Use SOP-01. |
| **3. Drivers, contractors and journeys** | Before each assignment or material change · Dispatch and Driver Leads | Check licences and entitlements, training and fitness under company policy. Apply applicable route, fatigue, work hours, load or weather controls. Verify outsourced providers and required evidence. Record the authorization or withhold dispatch and escalate. |
| **4. Inspect, hold and release** | Before use and on return to service · Driver, Workshop and Authorized Reviewer | Complete the class-specific walkaround. Record defects and place a hold where required. Verify current legal and operating evidence, mechanics and open holds. Require a named reviewer, release checks and timestamp to authorize deployment. Retain the release record. |
| **5. Maintenance, recalls and breakdowns** | By schedule, mileage, defect or notice · Maintenance Lead | Plan service and recalls, document work and verify contractor delivery. Confirm the repair scope and quality checks. Record a road or functional test where applicable. Escalate repeat faults or missed milestones. Retain work order, parts, QA evidence and updated recall or service status. Use SOP-03, SOP-04, SOP-05 and SOP-09. |
| **6. Registrations, inspections and insurance** | Daily and before release · Compliance Lead | Review Daily Command, apply the correct country's requirements, and assign owners for inspection and renewal separately where appropriate. Escalate missed deadlines. Confirm the replacement evidence matches the vehicle. Record the accepted document reference and expiry, then recheck release eligibility. Use SOP-07. |
| **7. Accidents, hazards and near misses** | At occurrence · Duty Manager and EHS Lead | Activate the site plan and make the area safe within your training. Log the EHS case and any linked Garage damage hold. Determine and record applicable authority, insurer and client reporting actions. Investigate causes, assign corrective actions and verify effectiveness before closure. Use EHS and SOP-09. |
| **8. Environmental controls** | During site operations and periodic review · Site Manager and EHS Lead | Check storage and spill readiness, segregate waste, monitor leaks and record fuel and energy. Verify authorised waste or contractor handling where applicable. Record exceptions, receipts and environmental incidents. Use EHS and SOP-06. |
| **9. Complaints and supplier quality** | On complaint, recurrence or service failure · Customer Experience and Quality Leads | Verify facts, assign recovery actions, record customer and supplier decisions and identify recurring causes. Route safety complaints to EHS or Garage. Retain communication, outcome and corrective action. Use SOP-03 and SOP-08. |
| **10. Operating and management reviews** | Daily, weekly, monthly and per management review schedule · Duty Manager and Director | Review holds, overdue actions and handovers daily. Review trends, recurring defects and owners weekly. Review cost and energy monthly. Record decisions, resources, deadlines and risk acceptance in management review. Use SOP-02 and SOP-06. |
| **11. Internal audit and corrective action** | Per approved audit programme and after significant findings · Quality Lead and Auditor | Set scope, criteria and samples. Observe the work and trace records to source evidence. Log findings, containment, cause, owner, deadline and effectiveness criteria. Require independent verification and reopen ineffective actions. |
| **12. Certification and follow-up** | At initial certification, surveillance and renewal · Compliance and Quality Leads | Complete the lifecycle below. Retain issuer decisions, findings and certificate scope. Escalate missed deadlines and scope changes. |

## 4. Escalation and closure

| Trigger | Immediate action and escalation | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Serious injury, fire, uncontrolled release or immediate danger | Stop unsafe activity, activate the site emergency plan and contact local emergency services as needed. Notify the Duty Manager and EHS Lead immediately. | Scene and incident timeline, containment, notification decisions, filing references, corrective actions and independent verification. |
| Unsafe vehicle, failed release gate, or overdue statutory inspection or registration | Prevent dispatch under the release procedure. Assign Compliance or Workshop and notify the Duty Manager within the shift as an internal target. | Accepted vehicle evidence, cleared defects where applicable and an authorized release decision. |
| High-risk near miss or contained significant hazard | Apply safe isolation. Notify the Site and EHS Leads within the shift and name an action owner. | Cause, controls, corrective action and effectiveness evidence. |
| Overdue follow-up, outstanding major finding or at-risk certification milestone | Notify the case owner and accountable manager. Quality or Compliance sets the recovery deadline and escalates unresolved high-risk items to the Director. | Dated recovery action, accepted evidence and reviewer sign-off. |

The lead records each applicable external deadline, its source, the allowed channel and the filing reference. Use the earlier or stricter applicable deadline if it differs from the internal target. Keep personal medical records in the authorised system and use approved references in shared cases.

## 5. Certification lifecycle

1. **Define scope and assess gaps.** Approve the entities, activities and sites. Map each relevant requirement to a control, SOP, accountable person and record. Assign gaps a severity, owner and due date.
2. **Operate the controls and collect evidence.** Train staff, apply the SOPs through representative operating cycles and collect records across the scope. Include exception decisions and corrective-action evidence.
3. **Audit internally and hold management review.** Sample vehicles, sites, release records, incident responses and supplier controls. For each finding, record evidence, cause, action and independent verification. The Director records the readiness decision and resources.
4. **Engage a certification body.** Check sector competence and relevant accreditation. Agree the scope, initial audit process, findings process, surveillance programme and renewal dates. [ISO certification guidance](https://www.iso.org/certification.html)
5. **Complete external audits and actions.** Prepare for the certification body's document and readiness review and audit of implementation. Track assigned findings, responses, evidence and the issuer's decisions.
6. **Verify the issued certificate.** Compare the legal entity, activities, sites, standard and edition with the agreed scope. Record the issuer and certificate number, issue and expiry dates, verification source, review date and named reviewer. Mark Verified active after independent issuer verification.
7. **Maintain, update and renew.** Track the next surveillance and recertification milestones per the issuer. Review changes in scope or status promptly. Use approved internal reminders, for example at 90, 60 and 30 days. The issuer's dates govern external milestones.

## 6. Records and performance

| Record | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Control and obligations register | Entity, country, site, activity, obligation or standard, current source, effective date, control, owner, evidence and review date |
| Certificate register | Standard and edition, scope, sites, issuer, accreditation reference where applicable, number, issue, expiry, surveillance and renewal dates, status, owner and verification |
| Audit and corrective action | Audit scope, criteria, auditor, sample and finding. Cause, containment, action, owner, deadline, evidence, effectiveness test and reviewer. |
| Vehicle release | Vehicle ID, class and jurisdiction, source revision, checks, hold status, evidence references, decision, reviewer and time |
| Incident and external notification | Case ID, risk or severity, owner, manager, due date, notification decision, channel, deadline source, filing reference, action and sign-off |
| Operating review | Period, measures, missed targets, trends, decisions, owners, deadlines and follow-up |

Review crashes and harm rates using a consistent mileage or journey denominator, preventive-maintenance compliance, overdue statutory cases, off-road hours, repeat defects, open audit actions, spills and fuel or energy intensity. The Director approves targets and review frequencies. Quality controls revision numbers, approvals, distribution, retention and access for records under the applicable company and jurisdiction requirements.
