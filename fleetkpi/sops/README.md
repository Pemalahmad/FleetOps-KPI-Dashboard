# `fleetkpi/sops/`: standard operating procedures

Doctrine law 5: *every flag has an owner within 48 hours.* Each SOP names its owner, cadence, trigger and checklist, and goes **Active** on the dashboard's SOPs page when the data trips its trigger.

| SOP | Owns | Triggered by |
|---|---|---|
| [SOP-01](SOP-01-data-onboarding.md) | Data onboarding | A new fleet or a failed validation |
| [SOP-02](SOP-02-weekly-ops-review.md) | Weekly ops review | Every week; backlog, time to pick up, back to operations |
| [SOP-03](SOP-03-service-center-intervention.md) | Site intervention | INTERVENE status, repeat repairs, key-to-key, parts on time |
| [SOP-04](SOP-04-recall-campaign.md) | Recall campaign | A recall launch or completion below target |
| [SOP-05](SOP-05-pm-compliance.md) | Preventive maintenance | PM compliance below target |
| [SOP-06](SOP-06-month-end-cost-energy.md) | Month-end cost & energy | Every month-end; cost against budget |
| [SOP-07](SOP-07-registration-insurance.md) | Registration, insurance & fines | Expired or expiring documents; fines per 100 vehicles |
| [SOP-08](SOP-08-customer-recovery.md) | Customer recovery & goodwill | 1-2 star ratings; goodwill above target |
