## Purpose
Complete every affected vehicle quickly and keep the evidence a regulator expects.

## Steps
- [ ] Load the campaign into `recalls` and the affected vehicle list into `recall_vehicles`.
- [ ] Brief every site on the procedure, parts and expected labour time.
- [ ] Book affected vehicles into planned downtime, prioritising the slowest regions on the completion curve.
- [ ] Track **completion % by region** weekly; chase regions lagging the curve.
- [ ] Hunt down the long tail: contact operators of vehicles not yet presented.
- [ ] Export the completion evidence (vehicle, date, work order) for the regulatory submission.

## Exit criteria
Completion at or above target; evidence pack filed.

## Escalation
A campaign below 70% at 12 weeks goes to the country manager.
