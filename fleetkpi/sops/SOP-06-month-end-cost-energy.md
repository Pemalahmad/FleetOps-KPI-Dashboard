## Purpose
Explain every variance, and steer the fleet mix using real total-cost-of-ownership evidence.

## Steps
- [ ] Review **cost vs budget** by region and explain every red cell (volume, price, or one-off).
- [ ] Compare **cost per km by powertrain**: energy, maintenance and depreciation.
- [ ] Check **energy cost per km** trends for price moves or efficiency drift (charging behaviour, idling).
- [ ] Review the **TCO outliers**: the 10 most expensive vehicles per km, and why.
- [ ] Track **CO₂ per km** against the fleet's emissions target.
- [ ] Feed the powertrain comparison into the next replacement or procurement decision.

## Exit criteria
Every red variance explained with an owner; the TCO view shared with procurement.

## Escalation
A region over budget for 2 consecutive months goes to the finance director, with a recovery plan.
