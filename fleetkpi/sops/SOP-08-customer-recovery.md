## Purpose
Turn every unhappy customer into a recovered one within 48 hours, and remove the cause so it does not repeat.

## Steps
- [ ] Pull yesterday's **1-2 star** ratings from Customer → ratings by site.
- [ ] Call each customer within 24 hours; log the root cause (delay, repeat fault, communication, cost).
- [ ] Check the linked work order: key-to-key time, repeat visit, parts wait.
- [ ] Agree a recovery action (re-inspection, courtesy vehicle, goodwill) and close the loop with the customer.
- [ ] Tag the root cause, and review the weekly totals in SOP-02.
- [ ] If one site drives the 1-2 star share, open SOP-03 for that site.
- [ ] Log every goodwill payment against its work order (`goodwill_amount`); goodwill is the price of a broken law, so name which one (repeat repair, missed key-back promise, parts delay).
- [ ] Monthly: if goodwill exceeds target as a share of service spend, take the top three causes to SOP-03.

## Exit criteria
Every 1-2 star rating contacted within 24 hours; 1-2 star share back under target.

## Escalation
Three 1-2 star ratings for the same root cause in a week go to the regional service lead.
