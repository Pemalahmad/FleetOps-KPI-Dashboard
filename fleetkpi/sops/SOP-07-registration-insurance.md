## Purpose
No vehicle drives unregistered or uninsured: protect the fleet from fines, impoundment and uninsured claims.

## Steps
- [ ] Open **Compliance → Renewal calendar** and list every vehicle Expired or Expiring in the next 30 days.
- [ ] Ground every **Expired** vehicle immediately: block it in dispatch until renewed.
- [ ] Batch Expiring renewals by insurer and authority; submit them at least 10 working days before expiry.
- [ ] Book any inspection the registration renewal needs into workshop capacity (Workshop → heatmap).
- [ ] Update the new expiry dates in the fleet register so the next build shows them **Valid**.
- [ ] Reconcile the insurer's schedule with the active fleet: every active vehicle on a policy, every disposed vehicle off it.

## Exit criteria
Zero Expired; registration and insurance valid rates at or above target.

## Escalation
Any vehicle Expired for more than 3 days goes to the country manager.
