## Reporting infringement

The repository and the web app are hosted by GitHub. You can either:

1. **Send the notice to GitHub**, which can act directly on hosted content: [github.com/contact/dmca](https://github.com/contact/dmca)
   (see [GitHub's DMCA Takedown Policy](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy)); or
2. **Contact us first**, privately, through the LinkedIn profile linked in the [README](README.md). Most issues (a
   missing attribution, a wrong licence line) are fixed fastest this way. Do not open a public issue containing
   personal details.

A valid notice includes:

1. Your physical or electronic signature, as the owner or a person authorised to act for the owner.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you say is infringed.
3. The exact location of the material: file path and commit, release asset, or page URL.
4. Your contact information.
5. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law.
6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised
   to act for the owner.

## Counter-notice

If material you contributed was removed and you believe that was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a
counter-notice with: your signature; identification of the removed material and where it appeared; a statement under
penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification; your name, address
and telephone number; and consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate court and to accept service from the person
who sent the original notice. Material may be restored 10–14 business days after a valid counter-notice unless the
complainant tells us they have filed a court action.

## What we do

On a valid notice we review promptly, remove or disable the material if warranted (including rewriting files in the
current branch and releases), tell the contributor where possible, and record the action. Removing material from git
history may require a history rewrite and can take longer.

## Repeat infringers

We remove the contributions of, and refuse further contributions from, anyone who repeatedly infringes copyright.

## Misrepresentation

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing, or was removed by mistake,
may be liable for damages. If you are unsure whether material infringes your rights, consider seeking legal advice
before sending a notice.
